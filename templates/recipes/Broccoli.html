Broccoli: The Guardian of Health


Broccoli, with its unique shape and abundant nutrients, serves as a culinary superhero. Rich in vitamin C, vitamin K, and fiber, it boosts immunity, supports bone health, and aids digestion. Its antioxidants fight free radicals, lowering the risk of chronic diseases. Whether steamed, boiled, stir-fried, or raw, broccoli offers nutritious options to protect and delight your body.

Boiled Broccoil


Step1

Wash the broccoli in cold water, pat it dry, peel the stem, and trim it right where the florets branch off. Break apart the florets.

Step2

Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Add the broccoli florets and cook, uncovered, until tender, which usually takes 2 to 3 minutes depending on the size of the florets.

Step3

Drain the boiled broccoli into a colander, transfer it to a plate, sprinkle it with salt and pepper, and serve with lemon wedges for extra flavor. Enjoy your delicious boiled broccoli !

Broccoil Salad


Step1

Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Chop broccoli into small pieces and prepare the dressing by whisking together olive oil, mayo, apple cider vinegar, mustard, maple syrup, garlic, and salt in a large bowl.

Step2

On the prepared baking sheet, toss almonds and pepitas with tamari, maple syrup, and smoked paprika. Spread them into a thin layer and bake for 10 to 14 minutes until golden brown. Let cool for 5 minutes.

Step3

Add baked almonds and pepitas to the broccoli salad along with onions and cranberries. Toss everything together, season to taste, and serve, garnishing with reserved almonds and pepitas. Enjoy!
