Mapo Tofu


Step1

Marinate ground pork or beef in a mixture of soy sauce and sugar. Drain and cut tofu into cubes, then simmer briefly and set aside.

Step2

Stir-fry ginger, garlic, and chopped scallions with fermented black beans, Sichuan chili bean paste, and ground Sichuan peppercorns. Add meat mixture, broth, and Shaoxing wine, then gently add tofu and boil until sauce reduces slightly.

Step3

Dissolve cornstarch in water, then pour into the pan and simmer until sauce thickens. Finish with toasted sesame oil, garnish with scallions, and serve over steamed rice.

Soymilk


Step1

Rinse, drain, and soak beans in water for 8 to 10 hours. Blend soaked beans with water until thick and creamy.

Step2

Set up a strainer lined with a tea towel over a mixing bowl. Heat blended mixture with additional water until foamy and beginning to boil. Pour hot mixture into the lined strainer.

Step3

Twist and press the towel to strain out soy milk. Return milk to the pot, simmer until sweetened, then add flavoring if desired. Serve warm or cold.
