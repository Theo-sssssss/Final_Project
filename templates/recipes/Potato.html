Unveiling Beef's Benefits: A Guide for High BMI Individuals


In the journey towards better health, choosing the right foods is crucial. For those with a high BMI, understanding how to make the right food choices is key. And beef, as a nutritious and versatile ingredient, may be just the excellent choice you need. Let's explore the allure of beef and how it can help you take solid steps on the path to wellness.

Beef: The World's Third Most Consumed Meat


Beef ranks third in global meat consumption, comprising about 25% of the market, behind pork (38%) and poultry (30%). Top consumers are the United States, Brazil, and China, with Argentina leading in per capita consumption at 64.6 kilograms annually. Major beef exporters include India, Brazil, Australia, and the United States, impacting economies like Paraguay, Argentina, Ireland, Mexico, New Zealand, Nicaragua, and Uruguay.

Cheese Baked Potatoes


Step1

Wash the potatoes, drizzle with vegetable oil, and sprinkle with salt. Bake them in the oven for approximately one hour until cooked through when a fork can easily pierce through the potato.

Step2

When the potatoes are easy to handle, cut an oval on the skin and remove them. Scoop out the inside of the potato using a spoon and place it in a mixing bowl. Add butter and blend using a potato masher.

step3

Mix the cheddar cheese, cooked chopped bacon, onion, sour cream, salt and pepper. Add it to the potato mixture, then stuff it into the scooped-out potatoes, Bake for 15 minutes, sprinkle with grated cheese, and bake for an additional 5-8 minutes until the cheese is melted.

Potato Salad


Step1

In a large pot, add potatoes, 1 tablespoon salt, and enough water to cover. Bring to boil, reduce heat, and simmer until potatoes are tender, about 10 minutes. Drain well and cool slightly.

Step2

Meanwhile, in a small bowl whisk together mayonnaise, sugar, vinegar, mustard, and garlic powder.

Step3

In a large bowl, add potatoes, onion, celery, and chopped eggs. Add mayonnaise mixture.Stir to combine and season to taste with salt and pepper (I like 1 ½ teaspoons salt and ¾ teaspoon pepper). Garnish with paprika.

Mashed Potatoes


Step1

Peel the potatoes according to the recipe instructions. Then, cut them into quarters and place them in a large pot of cold salted water. Using cold water helps ensure that the potatoes cook evenly.

Step2

Cook potatoes in boiling water until tender. The length of time you need to boil the potatoes depends on how big they are cut. I cut my potatoes into quarters and boil them for about 15 minutes. To check if your potatoes are ready, use a fork to poke the potato and see if it is tender!

Step3

Once well-drained, mash with the potatoes with a hand masher and with melted butter, warmed milk, and salt & pepper.
