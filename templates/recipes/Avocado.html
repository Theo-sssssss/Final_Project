Avocado Salad


Step1

Start by cutting the avocado into cubes and preparing other vegetables like lettuce, tomatoes, onions, and cucumbers.

Step2

Combine the diced avocado and veggies in a bowl. Add your choice of dressing, toss gently until coated evenly.

Step3

Transfer the salad to plates or bowls, garnish as desired with toppings like sesame seeds or cilantro, then enjoy your delicious avocado salad!

Avocado Sandwich


Step1

Prepare the avocado spread: Start by cutting a ripe avocado in half. Remove the seed and scoop out the flesh into a bowl. Mash the avocado with a fork and add finely chopped onion, cilantro, green chilies, garlic, black pepper, salt, and lemon juice. Mix until well combined.

Step2

Assemble the sandwich: Evenly spread the avocado mixture onto lightly buttered bread slices.

Step3

Toast the sandwich: Place the assembled sandwich on a skillet, frying pan, electric toaster, or panini press. Toast until golden brown and crispy. Enjoy your delicious avocado sandwich!
