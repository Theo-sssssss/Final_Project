Unveiling Beef's Benefits: A Guide for High BMI Individuals


In the journey towards better health, choosing the right foods is crucial. For those with a high BMI, understanding how to make the right food choices is key. And beef, as a nutritious and versatile ingredient, may be just the excellent choice you need. Let's explore the allure of beef and how it can help you take solid steps on the path to wellness.

Beef

Beef: The World's Third Most Consumed Meat


Beef ranks third in global meat consumption, comprising about 25% of the market, behind pork (38%) and poultry (30%). Top consumers are the United States, Brazil, and China, with Argentina leading in per capita consumption at 64.6 kilograms annually. Major beef exporters include India, Brazil, Australia, and the United States, impacting economies like Paraguay, Argentina, Ireland, Mexico, New Zealand, Nicaragua, and Uruguay.

Broiled Sirloin Steak


Step1

Preheat the broiler to high, 500 degrees F, with the top rack of the oven about 6 inches from the heat source. Sprinkle both sides of the steaks generously with salt and pepper. Place on a baking sheet and broil for 5 to 7 minutes on each side until they reach an internal temperature of at least 145 degrees F for medium.

Step2

While the steaks cook, melt 4 tablespoons of the butter in a large deep skillet over medium heat. Trim the toughest part of the stems from the maitakes and gently pull them apart into smaller pieces. Add the maitake mushrooms and shitake mushrooms to the butter. Cook, stirring often for about 3 minutes, until softened.

step3

Sprinkle in the oregano and thyme. Remove from the heat and add the remaining tablespoon of butter. Stir until it is melted. Add 1 teaspoon of salt and ¼ teaspoon black pepper, or an amount to meet your tastes.Once the steaks rest for about 3 minutes, top with the mushrooms and spoon any remaining butter over the meat. Sprinkle with chopped herbs and slice the meat to serve.

Braised Beef (lean only)


Step1

Preheat the oven to 350°F and position a rack in the middle. Prepare the vegetables: trim and halve 1 medium fennel bulb lengthwise, then dice into small pieces (about 1 1/2 cups). Dice 1 medium yellow onion (about 2 cups). Peel and slice 3 large carrots into 1/4-inch-thick rounds (about 2 cups). Finely chop 4 garlic cloves. Finally, trim and quarter 1 pound of cremini mushrooms.

Step2

Trim and cut 5 pounds boneless chuck roast into 3 to 4-inch pieces. Season with 1 tablespoon kosher salt, 1 1/2 teaspoons black pepper, and coat with 1/4 cup all-purpose flour. Heat 2 tablespoons vegetable oil in a Dutch oven, sear beef until browned on all sides, about 2 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate. Repeat in batches.

Step3

Reduce heat to medium-low. Sauté fennel, onion, and carrots until tender. Add garlic and tomato paste, cook briefly. Pour in red wine and beef broth, scraping up browned bits. Add mushrooms. Return beef to pot, nestling into vegetables and liquid. Adjust liquid level if needed with remaining beef broth.

Stir-Fry Beef


Step1

Heat the large wok/pan over medium-high heat and add 3 tbsp of oil and add the marinated beef. Flatten the beef and let it sear for 2-3 minutes or until the bottom part is charred. Then flip the beef and continue to stir fry for another 1-2 minutes or until no longer pink.

Step2

Add chopped garlic and follow the crushed chillies, onions, and bell peppers. Then pour the sauce into the wok and continue to stir fry for about 1 minute or until the sauce is glossy.

Step3

Add spring onions and make a quick toss. Finish with toasted sesame seeds and serve immediately with plain rice or noodles.
