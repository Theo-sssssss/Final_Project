Banana Milkshake


Step1

Begin by adding 2 ripe bananas. Optionally, include 20 to 24 cashews for added protein. You may skip cashews or substitute them with 2 tablespoons of nut butter or 6 scoops of vanilla ice cream. Cashews lend a rich and creamy texture to the shake. Optionally, soak them for 3 to 4 hours for added health benefits.

Step2

Skip this step if using ice cream. Sweeten the shake according to your taste preference with sugar or honey, typically around 2 tablespoons. Alternatively, opt for 4 pitted dates for natural sweetness. Be cautious, as excess dates can alter the shake's color. Enhance the flavor with ½ teaspoon of ground cinnamon or ¼ teaspoon of cardamom powder.

Step3

Pour in 1½ cups of chilled milk. If incorporating ice cream, reduce the milk to half a cup. Blend all ingredients until a smooth consistency is achieved. Adjust the thickness by adding more milk for a thinner shake or additional bananas for a thicker texture.

Tropical Fruit Salad


Ingredients

1 pineapple, cut into bite-sized pieces, 1 papaya, cut into bite-sized pieces, 1 mango, cut into bite-sized pieces, 1 banana cut into bite-sized pieces

Step1

Arrange fresh fruit in a bowl. Mix flavor enhancer ingredients until honey dissolves, then pour over the fruit and gently stir.

Step2

Portion fruit into small bowls, garnish with toasted coconut, and serve promptly.
