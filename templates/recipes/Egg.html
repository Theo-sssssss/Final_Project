Introduction

Eggs, originating from chickens, are encapsulated ovules with a hard shell. They contain egg white and yolk, crucial for nutrition, protected by various membranes. Fresh eggs are denser than water, causing them to sink, but as they release carbon dioxide over time, their density decreases, causing older eggs to float .

Description

An egg is a protective shell encasing albumen and yolk, rich in nutrients. Its structure safeguards life's beginning and facilitates gas exchange. This simple yet vital design symbolizes both fragility and resilience, serving as a nutritional cornerstone worldwide.

Nutrition

Eggs are rich in high-quality protein, vitamins such as B12 and riboflavin, minerals like selenium, and essential nutrients like vitamin D and choline. Despite concerns about cholesterol, they offer numerous health benefits and are a versatile addition to a balanced diet.
